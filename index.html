FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Instagram account?

No account is required by InstaFetch. Retrieval is limited to content that can be accessed without bypassing authentication or access controls.

Can I download private content?

No. InstaFetch is designed for publicly accessible content and does not bypass private-account protections.

Is InstaFetch free?

The frontend is free to use. Actual operating costs will depend on backend infrastructure, bandwidth and traffic.
